Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy describes the information that may be processed when you use AI Clipping Factory and connect a social media account.

Information processed

  • Account information and basic profile identifiers made available by a social platform when you authorize a connection.
  • Authorization credentials or tokens needed to maintain the connection and perform actions you request.
  • Video files, captions, titles, descriptions, and other publishing details that you provide for processing.
  • Operational records such as upload status, publication identifiers, and error information needed to prevent duplicate submissions and troubleshoot the service.

How information is used

Information is used to prepare the clips you request, connect to social platforms, submit content when you initiate or enable publishing, maintain publishing status, and troubleshoot errors.

Sharing

When you connect a social platform and request publishing, the relevant content and required account authorization are transmitted to that platform. We do not describe this as a sale of personal information.

Storage and security

Information may be stored in the environments used to operate the workflow, including local devices and connected cloud services. Appropriate access controls should be maintained. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Retention and choices

You can stop using the workflow and revoke a platform connection through that platform's account settings. Publishing records and files may remain in the storage locations you control until you delete them.

Children

This service is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The latest version will be posted on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact method provided by the operator of the AI Clipping Factory service.
